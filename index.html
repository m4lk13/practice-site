Services for each stage of the route to market.

What I do for companies that are entering the Russian and EAEU life-sciences market or already work in it. We agree the exact scope and deliverables for each project.

Stage 1 of 4

Assess the market

Market size, demand forecast and pricing scenarios.

Market research and financial modelling

Market sizing, forecasts, pricing scenarios and business cases you can take to a decision. Analysis of pharma market segments.

Distinctive strength

Digital analytics where IT meets healthcare

Interactive dashboards and automated pipelines that collect and process data from tenders (ЕИС), customs statistics and open registries, plus predictive models of access and demand.

Stage 2 of 4

Register the product

Registration pathways and a dossier that holds up under review.

Regulatory and market-access advice for pharma

Registration pathways in Russia and the EAEU, reimbursement listing (ЖНВЛП) and localisation requirements for pharmaceutical products.

Medical-device registration and compliance

Roszdravnadzor and EAEU pathways for devices: technical files, conformity assessment and post-market vigilance documentation.

Regulatory documentation and translation

Dossier support, including eCTD structure, technical and medical translation RU ↔ EN, and document preparation.

Stage 3 of 4

Prove the value

Pharmacoeconomics and HTA

Cost-effectiveness and budget-impact analysis, value dossiers and evidence packages for reimbursement and formulary decisions.
